Before you choose motorisation.

A few practical questions homeowners often ask before booking a home visit.

Some existing curtains and blinds can be adapted for motorised control, depending on the track, mechanism and fabric weight — others are better replaced for a cleaner, more reliable result. Your advisor can assess your existing treatment during a home visit and recommend the right approach.

Motorised curtains and blinds can be operated by remote control, app-based control, or compatible smart-home systems where appropriate. Your advisor can talk you through which option suits your home.

This depends on the specific motor and system chosen for your home. Your advisor can explain the power requirements — and any backup options — for the system recommended for your windows.

Motorised systems are designed to move smoothly and discreetly, so the operation stays in the background rather than drawing attention — see it in the video above. Noise levels vary by motor and system, and your advisor can advise further based on the option chosen for your home.
