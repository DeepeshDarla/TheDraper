About The Draper

Your windows, styled around your home.

A home-first window styling service for Mysore. We make the process of choosing curtains and blinds more personal, practical and considered.

Our approach

Good window design starts with the room.

Instead of choosing in isolation, we look at your light, privacy, furniture, colours and daily routine. Then we help you narrow down the right treatment and finish.

From the first home visit to final installation, the aim is simple: make your windows feel like they belong there. Explore our curtains and blinds collections to see what's possible in your own home.

Meet The Draper

Someone who knows windows. And knows homes.

A Draper advisor comes to you — not to a showroom, but to the room you're actually furnishing. They bring fabric, samples and a working knowledge of how curtains and blinds behave in real Mysore homes, and look at your light, privacy, furniture, colours and how you actually live before recommending anything.

It's a considered conversation, not a script, and there's no pressure to decide on the spot — just an honest read on your home and your choices, so that whatever you pick feels right once it's actually up.

This is what The Draper means by “the showroom comes home.”

From here, it's a short step to precise measurement, in-house tailoring and professional installation.
